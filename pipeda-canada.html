Local-First Financial Data & Payment Detection – Privacy & PIPEDA

ZenSave is a personal finance, salary-cycle budgeting, and savings-goal application operated by IgniSquare. Although ZenSave operates natively in English and Spanish, it is published globally on Google Play. The following explains how personal and financial data are handled for Canadian residents in compliance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

PIPEDA Compliance Across the 10 Principles

  • Accountability & Openness (Principles 1 & 8): IgniSquare is responsible for any personal data under its control and clearly distinguishes in this notice and the main Privacy Policy between data that remains solely on your phone and data optionally synced to Google Firebase.
  • Purpose Identification (Principle 2): Payroll settings, budgets, expenses, savings goals, and wishlists stay on your device by default solely to calculate your safe daily margin and projections. Optional Google Sign-In and ZenSave Pro cloud backup exist solely for account sign-in, lifetime purchase recovery, and optional device restoration.
  • Consent (Principle 3): Using ZenSave does not require creating an account. Optional features—including Google Sign-In, Automatic Payment Detection from device notifications, Pro biometric app lock, and system reminders—require your explicit activation and can be turned off anytime in Settings or Android system settings.
  • Limiting Collection (Principle 4): ZenSave contains no ads and no ad tracking. We never ask for banking passwords, GPS location, camera access, or Health Connect permissions. Installed apps are checked strictly locally on your device solely to let you choose which apps to monitor for payment detection, and that list is never uploaded.
  • Limiting Use, Disclosure & Retention (Principle 5): Payment notification reading and quick-entry clipboard amount checks run 100% locally in device memory, automatically discarding verification codes, OTPs, PINs, tokens, and promotions; raw messages never leave your phone. ZenSave never sells or rents personal or financial data. General app crash and performance diagnostics exclude financial balances and transaction entries.
  • Accuracy (Principle 6): You can view, edit, reconcile, or delete any salary setting, income, expense, fixed bill, savings goal, or wishlist item directly inside ZenSave at any time.
  • Safeguards (Principle 7): All network communications use encrypted HTTPS connections and block untrusted inspection certificates. Optional cloud records in Firebase are encrypted at rest by Google Cloud/Firebase, restricted per authenticated user, protected by platform integrity checks and anti-abuse rate limits, while device biometric locking is handled directly by Android's built-in security hardware without ZenSave reading biometric templates.
  • Individual Access & Challenging Compliance (Principles 9 & 10): You can inspect all data directly in the app, export your financial records to CSV or Excel (ZenSave Pro), delete your account and cloud backup instantly in-app, or contact ignisquare@gmail.com with inquiries or complaints.

Data Handled & Minimum Age Safeguard (+16)

  • Local Device Storage: Salary settings, currency preferences, incomes, expenses, fixed bills, savings goals, and app preferences stay in private storage on your phone with Android's generic system auto-backup disabled.
  • Optional Cloud Account & Pro Backup (Firebase): If you sign in with Google, we store basic Google profile details, consent confirmation timestamps, lifetime Pro license records, and—for Pro users—optional financial backup snapshots.
  • Minimum Age Requirement (+16 / Child Protection): ZenSave requires users to confirm they are 16 years or older before entering any financial information and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Account Deletion, Retention & Individual Rights Under PIPEDA

You remain in full control of both local and optional cloud data:

  • Instant In-App Cloud & Local Erasure (signed-in users): Go to Settings → Security & Privacy → Cloud Account → Delete Account and confirm after the 10-second countdown. ZenSave immediately and irreversibly deletes your cloud profile, cloud backup, consent record, and Pro cloud license link, deletes your Firebase authentication account, signs out Google on the device, and wipes local financial data and caches (retaining only a local 7-day anti-abuse cooldown record until uninstall).
  • Local-Only Users (no Google account linked): No personal or financial records exist on external servers; uninstalling ZenSave or clearing app data in Android Settings erases everything on the device immediately.
  • Access, Correction, Portability, or Complaints: Contact ignisquare@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 business days as required under PIPEDA.
